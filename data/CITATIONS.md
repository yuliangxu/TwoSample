# Microbiome data citations

The real data were obtained through **curatedMetagenomicData**, then subset,
normalized over 123 retained taxa, and exported at float32 precision. Please
cite the package and the four contributing studies below. Counts refer to
observations, not unique participants.

| Package study label | Training | Testing | Total | Reference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| HMP_2019_ibdmdb | 751 | 180 | 931 | Lloyd-Price et al. (2019) |
| HMP_2019_t2d | 210 | 67 | 277 | Zhou et al. (2019) |
| HallAB_2017 | 171 | 38 | 209 | Hall et al. (2017) |
| HanniganGD_2017 | 34 | 7 | 41 | Hannigan et al. (2018) |

1. **Package:** Pasolli E, Schiffer L, Manghi P, et al. Accessible, curated
   metagenomic data through ExperimentHub. *Nature Methods*. 2017;14:1023–1024.
   [doi:10.1038/nmeth.4468](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4468).
2. **IBDMDB:** Lloyd-Price J, Arze C, Ananthakrishnan AN, et al. Multi-omics of
   the gut microbial ecosystem in inflammatory bowel diseases. *Nature*.
   2019;569:655–662.
   [doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1237-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1237-9).
   Public sequence accession: **PRJNA398089**.
3. **T2D:** Zhou W, Sailani MR, Contrepois K, et al. Longitudinal multi-omics of
   host–microbe dynamics in prediabetes. *Nature*. 2019;569:663–671.
   [doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1236-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1236-x).
   Public microbial data: NIH HMP2/HMPDACC and the study's iPOP resources.
4. **Hall:** Hall AB, Yassour M, Sauk J, et al. A novel Ruminococcus gnavus
   clade enriched in inflammatory bowel disease patients. *Genome Medicine*.
   2017;9:103.
   [doi:10.1186/s13073-017-0490-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-017-0490-5).
   Cohort sequence accession: **PRJNA385949**.
5. **Hannigan:** Hannigan GD, Duhaime MB, Ruffin MT IV, Koumpouras CC,
   Schloss PD. Diagnostic Potential and Interactive Dynamics of the Colorectal
   Cancer Virome. *mBio*. 2018;9(6):e02248-18.
   [doi:10.1128/mBio.02248-18](https://doi.org/10.1128/mBio.02248-18).
   Sequence accession: **PRJNA389927**. The package label contains 2017;
   the publication year is 2018.

We acknowledge the original participants, investigators, and data-producing
consortia, including the NIH Integrative Human Microbiome Project, and the
curatedMetagenomicData maintainers for making these public resources available.
Source data retain their applicable terms; these references do not assign a
